## Description (4000 char max)

Old wisdom holds that a mother's blood renews every three years, a father's every
four — and whichever is freshest at the moment of reckoning is said to be the one
the child favors.

Old Wives' Reveal takes that old folk method and reckons it properly: enter both
parents' birth dates and a date to reckon from, and it works out exactly where each
parent sits in their renewal cycle, which one is "fresher," and what that's said to
mean.

FEATURES
- Enter both birth dates and get an instant reading — boy, girl, or a rare tie
- See each parent's cycle as a dial, with the exact math behind the reading
- Browse how the reading shifts over time, month by month
- Share a reading as a card image with anyone
- Keep a private history of past readings on your device

This is entertainment, not medicine. A baby's biological sex is set by chromosomes
at conception, regardless of what any folk method says — this app is a fun way to
explore an old tradition, not a prediction tool. We say so, right on the home
screen.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Just an old wives' tale, reckoned properly.
